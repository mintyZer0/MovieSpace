The Batman: This movie has too many shadows

This movie is crazy dark. Literally. I had to turn the brightness on my screen all the way up because the sun does not exist in Gotham City. Did you know that Batman is a bat? And a man? But unlike a real bat, he cannot fly and he does not sleep upside down. He is just a very rich guy in a Halloween costume beating people up in dark alleys.

So the story is about Bruce Wayne. He is a billionaire, which means he has a lot of money. Instead of going to therapy like a normal person, he spends his billions on a tank and bat-shaped throwing stars. Why bat shapes? Because branding is very important when you are fighting crime at 3 in the morning.

Then we have the city of Gotham. It is always raining in Gotham. Every single day, 24/7, non-stop rain. I think their drainage system must be amazing because the streets never flood. Batman walks around in heavy armor, so he must be soaking wet the entire movie. That sounds very uncomfortable.

The villain in this movie leaves riddles. Why? Because he loves puzzles. If the villain just sent a normal text message, the police would catch him immediately. But because he leaves cards with question marks, everyone has to spend two hours thinking about it.

Overall, I give this movie 10 out of 10 bats. If you like the color black and people whispering in low voices, this is the film for you. Highly recommend turning on the lights in your room while watching.
