Spider-man: This movie is absolutely bonkers

This movie is crazy good. Did you know that it has a spider? And a guy? Put them together, and you get a Spider-man. Big surprise, right? You would never guess that a guy named Spider-man has spider powers, but here we are.

So the story is about this guy named Peter Parker. And he wears a suit that is red and blue. Why? Because red and blue are colors. He shoots webs from his hands to swing on tall buildings. If the buildings were short, he would hit the floor, and that would hurt. Luckily, New York has tall buildings, so he doesn't die.

Then there are the bad guys. Bad guys in movies do bad stuff, which makes a lot of sense. When a bad guy shows up, Spider-man fights them. If he didn't fight them, the movie would just be him sitting around doing his homework, and that would be boring.

The movie looks very nice. When things blow up, there is fire and it is loud. When it is night time, it gets dark because the sun goes away. Very smart.

Overall, I give this movie 10 out of 10 spiders. If you have eyes, you can watch it. If you have ears, you can hear it. Go watch it right now.
